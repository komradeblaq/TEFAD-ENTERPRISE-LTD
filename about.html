Our Story

Creating memorable moments through food and service.

TEFAD Enterprise Ltd was created from a passion for excellent food, beautiful presentation and celebrations that leave lasting memories.

Based in Abuja Karu, Nigeria, we provide professional catering, custom cakes, pastries, small chops, event decoration and event-planning services for private and corporate clients.

Every client and occasion is unique. We therefore take time to understand each request, recommend suitable solutions and carefully execute every agreed detail.

From an intimate birthday celebration to a wedding or corporate gathering, our goal remains the same: to provide exceptional taste, dependable service and an experience people will be delighted to remember.

“We believe beautiful celebrations begin with thoughtful planning, excellent taste and genuine care.”
